💡 20-Point Decision Questions (DP1 - DP3)

Detailed justification prepared for evaluation.

📌 DP1: The Nudge — What does the app do when weekly target is crossed?

Decision: Constructive Warning with Actionable Offsets.
Why not shame? Psychological research shows shaming induces guilt and leads to users abandoning the app. Why not block? Blocking disables truth-seeking; if a user takes an emergency flight, blocking stops honest tracking. Instead, we display a clear banner stating the overshoot amount, pinpointing the primary category driver, and offering 3 micro-actions (e.g. 2 vegetarian meals or transit swaps) to balance it out.

📌 DP2: Absurd Input — Treating entries like a 500,000 km car trip?

Decision: Soft Validation with Contextual Warning.
We set physical single-entry thresholds (e.g., >1,500 km for a car, >200 kWh for daily electricity). If exceeded, the app displays a prominent confirmation notice asking the user to double check decimal errors (like typing 5000 instead of 50.0) without crashing or hard-blocking power users who may be logging fleet mileage.

📌 DP3: The Week — When does a week start, and how is mid-week pace shown?

Decision: Rolling 7-Day Window with Continuous Pace Tracking.
Fixed Monday-Sunday boundaries cause "Monday Reset Syndrome" (users who overshoot by Thursday give up for Friday/Saturday). A rolling 7-day window keeps continuous accountability on any day.
